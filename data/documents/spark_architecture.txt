Apache Spark is a distributed data processing engine designed to process large datasets across a cluster.

A Spark application consists of a driver process and executor processes. The driver coordinates the application, creates the execution plan, and schedules work. Executors run tasks assigned by the driver and store data for computation.

When an action is triggered, Spark creates a job. The job is divided into stages based on shuffle boundaries. Stages are further divided into tasks. A task is the smallest unit of work sent to an executor.

Spark uses partitions to divide a dataset into smaller pieces that can be processed in parallel. Each task normally operates on one partition. The scheduler assigns tasks to available executor cores.

Executors can run multiple tasks concurrently depending on the number of cores allocated to them. This parallel execution allows Spark to process large datasets across multiple machines.

Shuffle operations move data between executors. Operations such as groupBy, join, and repartition can introduce shuffle boundaries and may require network and disk I/O.
