Data quality checks are used to identify incorrect, incomplete, inconsistent, or unexpected data before it affects downstream consumers.

Completeness checks verify that required fields are populated and that expected records are present.

Uniqueness checks detect duplicate values in columns that should contain unique identifiers.

Validity checks verify that values conform to expected formats, ranges, or allowed categories.

Referential integrity checks verify that foreign keys have corresponding records in related dimension or reference tables.

Freshness checks determine whether data arrived within the expected time window.

Row-count checks compare the number of records against expected thresholds or historical patterns.

Data quality failures should be observable and actionable. A pipeline can record failed checks, affected datasets, batch identifiers, and error details.

Quarantine mechanisms can isolate invalid records while allowing valid records to continue through the pipeline when the business process permits it.

Quality metrics can also be used for monitoring trends over time, helping teams identify gradual deterioration in source data.
